Chapter I

It started rather unexpectedly.

Our very first conversation was born from a rather questionable little mission, trying to find your social media profile.

“I found it… so I think I deserved talking to you.”

And somehow, that small attempt led me here, remembering a person I never expected would become such a meaningful part of my story.

Chapter II

For a while, the road felt clear.

We were only talking at first. But somewhere along the way, our conversations became something more than casual words.

We talked about the future. About possibilities. About things that, at the time, felt like they might actually happen.

Some conversations stay with you, even after the conversation ends.
Chapter III

Then the road changed.

Things didn't turn out the way I once imagined. And I understand that.

You told me that you couldn't reciprocate my feelings, and although that wasn't what I hoped to hear, I respect you for being honest with me.

Some roads simply aren't meant to end at the same destination.

Chapter IV

But some things remain.

Your smile.

The way you can be so passionate about running, always moving forward, one step after another.

And your love for books, which somehow feels fitting, because I think some people become chapters you never really stop remembering.

You became a chapter I didn't know I would carry with me.
Chapter V

And then came September 9.

It is my birthday.

Every birthday I've had was another opportunity for me to give something to the people I care about.

So this year, instead of keeping the day entirely for myself, I wanted to give a small part of it to you.

A small portion of the space you still occupy inside my heart.
